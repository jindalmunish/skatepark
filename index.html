⭐ FLAGSHIP FACILITY • #1 IN INDIA

Chandigarh Skatepark

📍 Tiranga Urban Park, Sub City Center, Sector 17, Chandigarh

India's premier championship concrete skate plaza. Engineered by 100 Ramps with 40,000 sq. ft. of smooth concrete surface, featuring pool coping, deep and shallow clover bowls, an 8-stair with square handrail, hubbas, euro gap, and evening floodlights.

Cultural Evolution

Historical Context: Three Defining Eras

From backyard ramps and underground basements to Olympic sports recognition.

2010 – 2015 Era I

Underground & DIY Genesis

When public parks were non-existent, pioneers built private backyard bowls (Play Arena Bengaluru, 2013) and weatherproof subterranean ramp rooms in Delhi (Delhi SB, Neb Sarai).

  • • Play Arena Concrete Bowl (Bengaluru, 2013)
  • • Delhi SB Subterranean Ramps (Delhi, 2016)
  • • The Cave Community DIY Bowl (Hegde Nagar)
Explore Era I Facilities →
2015 – 2020 Era II

Rural Social Catalyst & Coastal Hubs

Skateboarding broke village caste lines at Janwaar Castle (MP), produced national champions at Desert Dolphin (Rajasthan), and established coastal plazas at Carter Road (Mumbai).

  • • Janwaar Castle Rural Social Bowl (Panna, MP)
  • • Desert Dolphin 3-Tier Clover Bowl (Khempur, RJ)
  • • Carter Road Seafront Plaza (Mumbai)
Explore Era II Facilities →
2021 – Present Era III

Olympic Era & Civic Megaparks

Olympic recognition catalyzed large municipal budgets: 44,000 sq. ft. megaparks in Chandigarh & Lucknow, pump tracks at 11,500 ft in Leh, and rooftop DIY spots like Empty Heads in Hyderabad.

  • • Chandigarh Sector 17 Plaza (40k sq ft)
  • • Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Skatepark (44k sq ft)
  • • Disko Valley High Altitude Pump Track (11,500 ft)
