Under-Investigation Spots & Community Leads

To guarantee 100% integrity on the primary Verified Directory, an entry requires verified GPS coordinates, builder confirmation (such as 100 Ramps), civic tenders, or verified news. The 12 locations below represent community-reported DIY spots, unconfirmed municipal announcements, and private setups awaiting ground proof.

Know of Another Park or Have Ground Proof?

If you skate at any of the unverified locations above, or know of a new public skatepark, concrete bowl, or pump track anywhere in India that is not yet on our registry, help us document it.

Required for verification: 1. Exact Google Maps Pin • 2. Ground photos or video of the ramps • 3. Builder name (if known) • 4. Whether access is public/free or paid.

Once verified, the spot will be assigned an official National Facility Code (e.g. IN-KA-05) and published directly to the master directory.
