IN-TG-02 Empty Heads Rooftop Skatepark (Secunderabad / Hyderabad)
Verified News Feature

Rolling on: Hyderabad's skaters carve their own path across rooftops and ramps

📰 The Times of India • 📅 October 2023

Spotlights Empty Heads as an independent, skater-built rooftop haven in the Alwal/Venkatapuram area of Secunderabad. Built by passionate local youth to counter the city's lack of municipal skateboarding plazas, the rooftop terrace features wooden mini-ramps and serves as an underground creative hub for Hyderabad's subculture.
